**Cycle of teaching**: Assessing and diagnosing learning process > derive adaptive teaching activities > Implement them > loop around

Their study found 3 types: systems for learning analysis, learning support, and customizing the learning environment 

1. Assessing: However, lots of media bridges from one platform to the next, dropoff. Limited followup or support. ex. classroom management who to pair students w based on where their testing is falling off. 
2. Adaptive: mostly intelligent tutoring systems, rule-based linking. 2400 exersizes. 200,000 tasks in Bettermarks. can provide feedback for right & wrong steps, support task selection as well as step loop. Hard to connect research to development, US focused. Cumbersome & expensive, content specific. 
3. Customizing Environment: Adapting materials, giving feedback, assistive tech. That's what they think they can currently do. Don't really have domain or pedagog. knowledge, not built w in mind. Teachers use GPTs to create tasks and questions, multiple choices. Summarize text for diff grade reading levels. They use a variety of low-level applications but not systematically. Lack of didactic and methodological pedagogical concepts. Need knowledge of limitations and challenges. No regulatory frameworks for what you can use in schools. 

Makes role of teacher even more important. Have to know why/how you're using the tool exactly. **Need to link research, development and deployment**

Good at providing practice opportunities, language learning, doing exercises, vocabulary. Not good at making sense, exploring, inquiring, giving the correct answer, checking the answer. 

Starting points: creating conditions, supporting use, encouraging further development of the tools. Not universal in Germany

Challenges: Dealing w data. How to use AI in Edu. 

Hard to communicate to policy that there are no definite answers. 

Rather than thinking of AI as in a replacement way, think in a complementing way, how to roles change, new skills around teaching with AI. 

Design sprints / data analysis sprints as a way to get diff domain knowledge involved / communities involved... for clearly defined sub problems around this issue 

HCI methods, solutions/storyboards, participatory formats. A teacher or two on deisn team from the getgo. Design sprints with students. 

Some software perpetuating bad teaching practices, catering to what the teachers wanted. 

**Helps to ask ppl "What are your problems? What can you use help with?" rather than what do you want what can you imagine the system can do for you — can be limited by what you image the system can do for you.** 




conversely, students trying to undermine learning activities with AI. Need to reconsider what our goals are, ways of making them learn it without those possibilities we had before. Too easy to use the Washing machine (AI)
